The Story

Hagar and the God who sees

Read: Genesis 16:1-13 →

Used, dismissed, and running through a desert nobody would cross for her — Hagar is the first person in the Bible to give God a name. She called Him El Roi: "the God who sees me." Not the God who sees the crowd. The God who found one invisible woman by a spring and knew her whole story.
