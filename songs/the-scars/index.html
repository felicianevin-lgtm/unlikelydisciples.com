The Story

Joseph, and the wound that fed nations

Read: Genesis 50:15-21 →

Betrayed by his brothers, sold, slandered, forgotten in a prison — and every one of those scars became the road to the throne room where he saved the very family that broke him. "You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good." Even the risen Jesus kept His scars — they're proof, not shame.
