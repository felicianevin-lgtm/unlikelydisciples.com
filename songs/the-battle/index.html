The Story

Jehoshaphat's choir that marched in front of the army

Read: 2 Chronicles 20:1-30 →

Three armies were coming and Judah had no chance. God's battle plan: "the battle is not yours, but God's — stand firm and see." So Jehoshaphat put the singers in FRONT of the soldiers, and as the praise went up, the enemy fell. They never drew a sword. That's what lifting your hands instead of clenching fists does.
