The Story

The long way out of Egypt

Read: Exodus 13:17-22 →

When Pharaoh finally let Israel go, there was a short road to the Promised Land — and God deliberately didn't take it. Scripture says He led them the long way around, by a pillar of cloud and fire, because the short road would have broken them. Some detours aren't delays. They're protection with a route.
