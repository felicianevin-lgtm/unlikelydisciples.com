The Story

Isaiah, the man with unclean lips

Read: Isaiah 6:1-8 →

Isaiah's first reaction to seeing God's glory wasn't volunteering — it was "woe is me, I'm ruined." Then the coal touched his lips, guilt taken, and the same mouth that said "I'm unclean" said "here am I — send me." God has never once called the equipped. He equips the called.
