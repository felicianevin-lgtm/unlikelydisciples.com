The Scripture

The verses this song is built on — sit with them slowly.

“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”

The Story

Elijah under the broom tree

Read: 1 Kings 19:3-8 →

Right after his greatest victory, Elijah hit the wall — exhausted, afraid, asking God to just let it end. God's answer wasn't a rebuke. It was sleep, fresh bread, and a gentle "the journey is too much for you." Sometimes the holiest thing you can do is rest and let Him feed you.
