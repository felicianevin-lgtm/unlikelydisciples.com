The Story

Peter, John, and the question that wasn't his to ask

Read: John 21:20-22 →

Fresh off his restoration, Peter looked over his shoulder at John and asked, "Lord, what about him?" Jesus' answer is the whole cure for comparison: "What is that to you? You follow Me." Your race has your name on it.
