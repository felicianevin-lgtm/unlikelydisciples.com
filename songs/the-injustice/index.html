The Story

David, Saul, and the sword he didn't swing

Read: 1 Samuel 24:1-12 →

David had his enemy cornered in a cave — the man hunting him unjustly, asleep and defenseless. One swing and it would be over. He cut the robe instead of the throat and said, "May the LORD judge between me and you." Taking your hands off the scales isn't weakness. It's trust.
