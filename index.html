The Name Is the Whole Point

About

Unlikely Disciples is Christian music for imperfect people — for anyone who's ever felt unqualified, overlooked, or ordinary and wondered if God could still use them. He can. He does. That's the whole point.

It started with one honest moment: I asked God… about the fear, the heartache, the silence, the scars. The answers that came back weren't lectures. They were invitations — to have faith, to trust, and to declare who He is. The debut album I Asked God walks through those prayers one at a time — the fear, the forgiveness, the comparison, the shame — and lands every single one on greater is He.

Because God's résumé is full of people nobody would hire. A murderer wrote the Law. A madam saved the spies. A traitor wrote the first Gospel. If He chose them, don't tell me He can't use you.

Unlikely vessels carrying His light — "We have this treasure in jars of clay to show that this all-surpassing power is from God and not from us." (2 Corinthians 4:7)

Honest lyrics. Real hope. Welcome, unlikely disciple.
